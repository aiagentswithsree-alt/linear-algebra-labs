Linear Algebra Labs

Learn-by-doing labs that follow 3Blue1Brown's Essence of Linear Algebra. In each mission you see the problem, predict, try it, then learn the maths.

Chapter 1
Vectors
Guide a delivery robot: coordinates, vector addition, scaling, position vs movement.
Chapter 2
Span, basis and linear dependence
Steer with two knobs: basis vectors, linear combinations, span in 2D and 3D, dependent vs independent.
Chapter 3
Linear transformations and matrices
Move the whole grid: linear or not, follow î and ĵ, matrix × vector, rotation and shear, squishing space.
Chapter 4
Matrix multiplication as composition
One move then another: composition, reading right to left, multiplying by following î and ĵ, AB ≠ BA, associativity.
