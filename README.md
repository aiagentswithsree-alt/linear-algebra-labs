# Linear Algebra Labs

Interactive, learn-by-doing labs that follow 3Blue1Brown's *Essence of Linear Algebra* series.
Each lab turns one chapter into short missions: **see the problem → predict → try it → learn the maths**.

## Labs

| Chapter | Lab | Concepts |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Vectors](ch01-vectors/index.html) | Coordinates, vectors as arrows and lists, vector addition, scalar multiplication, position vs movement, 3D |
| 2 | [Span & basis](ch02-span/index.html) | Basis vectors î and ĵ, changing basis, linear combinations, span in 2D and 3D, linear dependence, definition of a basis |
| 3 | [Transformations & matrices](ch03-transformations/index.html) | Transformations as movement, linearity test, where î and ĵ land, matrix–vector multiplication, rotation, shear, squishing onto a line |
| 4 | [Composition](ch04-composition/index.html) | Composition of transformations, matrix product read right to left, columns of a product, AB ≠ BA, associativity |

## Step-through lessons (trial)

A lighter, read-and-watch version of each chapter: an animated diagram, one short step at a time, with a running total and optional quick checks.

| Lesson | Topic |
|---|---|
| 1 | [What is a vector?](lessons/ch01-vectors/index.html): one warehouse robot, one order (about 12 min) |

## How each lab guides you

- **📘 What you'll learn**: opens automatically on your first visit (and anytime from the header): goals, why it matters, time needed, prerequisites, the mission list and the big-picture thread.
- **🎯 Goal and 🛠 How** at the top of every mission.
- **💡 Need help?** anytime, in three levels: a nudge, a bigger hint, then a full worked solution.
- **📖 Glossary** of key terms up to that chapter.
- **🌍 In real life**: a real-world use shown after each mission.

## How to use

Open `index.html` (chapter list) or `ch01-vectors/index.html` in any browser. No install or internet needed apart from web fonts.
Progress is saved in your browser.

## Chapter 1 missions

1. **Find the parcel**: coordinates (video 2:41–3:56)
2. **Command the robot**: vector = length + direction (0:30–0:44, 3:22–3:48)
3. **Combine two moves**: vector addition (4:36–6:45)
4. **Stretch, shrink, flip**: scalar multiplication (6:54–8:00)
5. **Fix the broken command**: position vs movement (goes beyond the video)
6. **Prove it**: randomised challenge, explain-why questions, 3D extension

## Chapter 2 missions

1. **Coordinates are knob settings**: basis vectors î and ĵ (video 0:11–1:42)
2. **Swap the arrows**: a different basis (1:47–3:02)
3. **Why "linear"?**: linear combinations (3:02–3:29)
4. **Where can it reach?**: span in 2D, vectors as points (3:29–5:52)
5. **The useless knob**: linear dependence (8:16–9:06)
6. **Span in 3D**: rotatable 3D view of planes and all of space (5:58–8:05)
7. **Prove it**: random rounds incl. an impossible one, and the basis puzzle (9:12–9:33)

## Chapter 3 missions

1. **Watch space move**: transformation = function, vectors as points (video 0:43–2:21)
2. **Linear or not?**: inspect four machines, including one that only a diagonal line exposes (2:39–3:23)
3. **Follow î and ĵ**: deduce where v lands from the basis vectors (3:35–5:58)
4. **Matrix × vector**: columns = where î and ĵ land (6:04–7:48)
5. **Build the matrix**: click to place î and ĵ for a rotation and a shear (8:00–8:55)
6. **Read a matrix**: matrix → picture, and dependent columns squish space (8:55–9:44)
7. **Prove it**: random matrix × vector rounds and a reverse round (whole video)

## Chapter 4 missions

1. **Warm-up**: recap of matrices as transformations (video 0:10–1:58)
2. **One move, then another**: rotate then shear, read the composition matrix (2:01–2:52)
3. **Long way vs short way**: S(Rv) = (SR)v, read products right to left (3:03–4:25)
4. **Multiply without watching**: follow î and ĵ through M₁ then M₂ (4:31–5:56)
5. **The general rule**: each column of AB is A × (column of B) (5:56–7:19)
6. **Does order matter?**: shear/rotate both ways, AB ≠ BA (7:28–8:12)
7. **Parentheses & challenge**: why (AB)C = A(BC), plus random products (8:21–9:42)
